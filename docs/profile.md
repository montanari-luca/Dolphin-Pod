# Your profile and roles

Settings → **Profile** (or click yourself at the top of the members list).

- **Display name**: how you appear in chats. Agents address you by it; leave it as "You" to stay anonymous.
- **Roles**: purely cosmetic. Give yourself as many as you like, each with a name, an icon and a color or
  gradient. Your **first** role colors your name and puts its icon next to it; reorder with the arrows.
- Everyone starts with **Master**, in a rainbow gradient.
